FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should we book?

As early as possible, especially for summer weekends and the WEF period. For smaller events a few weeks is often enough: get in touch and we will check availability.

Are set-up and take-down included?

Yes. We handle transport, set-up, testing and take-down. On-site support during the event is agreed together in the quote.

Can we show our own photos and videos on the screens?

Of course. Send us photos, videos or logos before the event: we prepare them and play them during the evening, alongside the live feed if you wish.

Where do you work?

Throughout Switzerland and, on request, in Italy, Germany and France. For outdoor events we assess the venue, power supply and weather protection together.

Can cold sparks and fog be used indoors?

In most venues, yes: cold sparks work without an open flame and we use professional equipment only. We always check the venue's rules and smoke detectors in advance.
